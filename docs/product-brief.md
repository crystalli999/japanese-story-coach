# Product brief

## Goal

Help one learner establish a sustainable 15-minute daily Japanese practice loop that adapts to demonstrated strengths and recurring mistakes and gradually expands from kana and N5 toward N1.

## First user

The initial product is personal and local-first. Broader distribution, accounts, synchronization, and publishing are future decisions rather than assumptions.

## Core experience

1. Take a thorough kana and N5 diagnostic.
2. Receive a short daily lesson based on the learner model.
3. Read an original BL-themed story or conversation using relevant grammar and vocabulary.
4. Complete comprehension, recall, and memorization exercises.
5. Update mastery, confidence, error patterns, and review scheduling.
6. See encouraging daily and weekly progress, including readiness against privately supplied manga samples.

## Content boundary

- Default to original generated or human-authored stories.
- Treat local manga scripts as private reference material until provenance and permitted use are reviewed.
- Do not publish or redistribute copyrighted manga lines by default.
- Record the source and usage rights of every curriculum dataset before importing it.

## Initial milestone

Design an N5-only prototype and prove the diagnostic → adaptive lesson → quiz → memory-update loop before expanding to N4–N1.

## Open product decisions

- Initial platform: web, native Apple, or cross-platform
- Local-only progress versus synchronization
- Reviewed story library versus live story generation
- Curriculum source and licensing
- Exact motivation mechanics and tone
